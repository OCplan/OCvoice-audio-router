OCvoice Audio Router

YOUTUBE · THIS COMPUTER

Connect the computer that sends your video

Keep this app open on the computer running OBS or your video program. It combines your video with OCvoice’s translated audio for your YouTube broadcasts.

Checking the app…

1

Connect this computer to OCvoice

In OCvoice, connect your YouTube channel and generate a one-time code to connect a computer. Enter that code here.

Open OCvoice ↗

To use another OCvoice account, stop the connection first and enter a new code.

2

Start the connection

Start the connection here. Then choose languages and create the translated broadcasts in OCvoice. Check their visibility before sending video.

A running connection does not mean video is arriving or YouTube is live. Check broadcast status and viewer links in OCvoice.

3

Send video from OBS

On this computer, open OBS → Settings → Stream. Choose Custom as the service and copy these fields. Use H.264 video. Start streaming in OBS after creating the broadcasts in OCvoice.

This is a local video label, not your YouTube stream key. The app does not find your camera automatically. Other video programs must support sending RTMP video to these same fields.

4

Check picture and translated sound

Open each YouTube viewer link from OCvoice. Check the language, picture and sound together. When finished, stop the broadcasts in OCvoice and stop streaming in OBS, then stop the connection here.

The app must stay open while broadcasting. Open this page again from the app’s “YouTube setup / Opsætning” menu.
